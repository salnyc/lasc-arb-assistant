I can help you three ways:

  • Answer questions about the ARB Standards & Design Guidelines — fences, sheds, decks, pools, lighting, landscaping, and more, with section references.
  • Draft your application — I'll ask for the details the ARB needs and produce text you can paste into the official form, plus a checklist of required attachments.
  • Give a preliminary read on whether your project appears consistent with the written guidelines before you submit.

This assistant is informational only and covers ARB topics exclusively. Nothing here is an approval or denial — only the ARB issues decisions, in writing, after reviewing a complete application (§2.7). The Guidelines are written in English; replies in other languages are unofficial translations provided for convenience, and the English text governs.
